Secure, Client-Side File & Document Conversion

Our browser-based file conversion utility offers fast, free, and completely private document processing. Unlike standard online converters that transmit your files to remote servers, all conversions take place directly within your device's browser memory.

Key Features & Capabilities:

  • PDF to Image Processing: Render individual pages from PDF documents into high-resolution PNG, JPEG, WEBP, or BMP format.
  • Word Document (DOCX) Conversion: Convert Microsoft Word documents into versatile web images or inspect document visual previews on the fly.
  • Google Docs Exporter: Parse public Google Doc web links to download documents in PDF, DOCX, TXT, or OpenDocument (ODT) formats.
  • Privacy First: Your confidential data never leaves your device. No file size tracking or server storage risks involved.

Add to Home Screen

Install File Converter directly to your mobile home screen or desktop for quick access and offline use across popular web browsers:

🌐 Google Chrome (Android / Desktop)

  1. Tap the three-dots menu icon (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  2. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  3. Tap Add / Install to confirm.

🌐 Safari (iPhone / iPad / Mac)

  1. Tap the Share icon (square with an up arrow) at the bottom toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap Add in the top right corner.

🌐 Opera Mini & Opera Mobile

  1. Tap the Opera logo (O) or three-dots menu in the navigation bar.
  2. Select "Add to Home screen" or tap the "+" (Plus) icon next to the address bar.
  3. Confirm by tapping Add.

🌐 Microsoft Edge

  1. Tap the three-dots menu (•••) at the bottom center or top right.
  2. Select "Add to phone" or "Install site as an app".
  3. Tap Add to save the web app.

🌐 Mozilla Firefox

  1. Tap the three-dots menu (⋮) next to the address bar.
  2. Select "Install" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Confirm by selecting Add automatically.

Open in Safari / Default Browser (iPhone)

In-app browsers (like Facebook, Instagram, or Telegram) can restrict file downloading and conversions. Here is how to open or set Safari as your default browser on iOS:

📱 Open Current Page in Safari

  1. Tap the three-dots icon (...) or Share icon (square with arrow up) at the bottom or top corner of your screen.
  2. Tap "Open in Safari" or "Open in Browser".

⚙️ Set Safari (or Chrome) as Default Browser in iOS Settings

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Scroll down and select Safari (or Chrome).
  3. Tap Default Browser App.
  4. Select Safari or your preferred browser app.

Help & Instructions

Converting Local Files:

  • Tap the drop zone or drag files into it.
  • Supported formats: Images (PNG, JPG, WEBP), PDF documents, Word DOCX, and TXT files.
  • Adjust width, height, and quality settings on the right panel before saving.

Converting Google Docs:

  • Switch to the "Google Doc URL" tab.
  • Paste the public link of your Google Doc and tap "Process Link".
  • Download the file directly as PDF, DOCX, TXT, or ODT.
