Add to Home Screen

Install this web app on your phone for quick access anytime!

1. Google Chrome (Android)

  1. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right corner.
  2. Select Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Follow the prompt to confirm.

2. Apple Safari (iOS / iPhone)

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an up arrow) at the bottom.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

4. Microsoft Edge (Android / iOS)

  1. Tap the three lines menu (≡) at the bottom right.

Help & User Guide

💱 Live Currency Exchange

Convert seamlessly between major African fiat currencies (NGN, ZAR, KES, GHS, etc.), global currencies (USD, EUR, GBP), and popular cryptocurrencies (BTC, USDT).

  • Step 1: Enter your desired amount in the input field.
  • Step 2: Select your base currency in the From dropdown.
  • Step 3: Select your destination currency in the To dropdown.
  • Step 4: Tap Convert Live to fetch the latest market rate.
  • Quick Swap: Tap ⇄ Swap Currencies to quickly switch source and target currencies.

📈 Investment Growth Calculator

Project the compound growth of your investments over time.

  • Enter your Starting Principal.
  • Set the estimated Annual Return Rate (%).
  • Specify the total duration in Years.
  • Tap Calculate Future Value to see your estimated compounded total.

🏦 Loan & EMI Re-payment Calculator

Determine your precise monthly installments (Equated Monthly Installment) for mortgages, personal loans, or business financing.

  • Input the total Loan Amount.
  • Enter the annual Interest Rate (%) charged by the lender.
  • Enter the payback duration in Months.
  • Tap Calculate Monthly Payment to generate your fixed EMI.

💾 Automatic State Saving

Your inputs, currency selections, and theme preferences are automatically saved locally on your device so you can resume calculations anytime without re-entering data.

📡 Offline Capabilities

The app automatically detects internet connectivity. While live rates require an active connection, both the Investment and Loan calculators work fully offline.

About

Exchange Currency Calculator

Exchange Currency Calculator is a comprehensive, mobile-first financial utility suite designed to streamline currency conversion, portfolio estimation, and loan calculations.

Core Capabilities

  • Live Exchange Rates: Instant conversion across African currencies, global fiat markets, and cryptocurrency assets.
  • Investment Forecasting: Compounded portfolio growth estimation.
  • Loan & EMI Calculations: Fixed monthly installment calculations.
  • Offline Support: Offline availability for math-based calculations.
  • Responsive & Dark Mode: Clean design optimized for mobile and desktop screens.

API & Data Sources

Real-time conversion feeds are powered by:

  • ExchangeRate-API: Global and African fiat exchange rates.
  • CoinGecko API: Live market valuation for cryptocurrencies.
