Add to Home Screen Instructions

🌐 Google Chrome (Android & Desktop)

  1. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  2. Select Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Confirm by clicking or tapping Add.

🧭 Apple Safari (iOS & iPadOS)

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an upward arrow) at the bottom.
  2. Scroll down the action sheet and select Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

🦊 Mozilla Firefox (Android)

  1. Tap the three dots (⋮) next to the URL bar.
  2. Tap Install or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm the prompt.

🌊 Microsoft Edge (Mobile & Desktop)

  1. Tap/click the three dots (...) menu button.
  2. Choose Add to phone (Mobile) or Apps > Install WebCraftStudio (Desktop).
  3. Confirm installation.

🔴 Opera Mini / Opera (Android)

  1. Tap the Opera icon or three dots menu in the browser bar.
  2. Select Home screen or tap + Add to.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen.
